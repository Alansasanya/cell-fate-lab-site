Nature Genetics 57, 2522–2535 · 2025

转录因子剂量如何塑造细胞重编程的异质性Dissecting the impact of transcription factor dose on cell reprogramming heterogeneity using scTF-seq

Liu, Wangjie; Saelens, Wouter; Rainer, Pernille; Biočanin, Marjan; Gardeux, Vincent; Gralak, Antoni Jakub; van Mierlo, Guido; Gebhart, Angelika; Russeil, Julie; Liu, Tingdang; Chen, Wanze; Deplancke, Bart

scTF-seq 将转录因子过表达剂量与单细胞转录组联系起来。研究构建了涵盖 384 个小鼠转录因子的功能图谱，揭示剂量与因子组合如何影响细胞状态转变。scTF-seq connects transcription factor dose with single-cell expression profiles. An atlas of 384 mouse transcription factors reveals how dose and factor combinations shape cell-state transitions.

Nature 608, 733–740 · 2022

记录同一细胞随时间变化的转录组Live-seq enables temporal transcriptomic recording of single cells

Chen, Wanze; Guillaume-Gentil, Orane; Rainer, Pernille Yde; Gäbelein, Christoph G.; Saelens, Wouter; Gardeux, Vincent; Klaeger, Amanda; Dainese, Riccardo; Zachara, Magda; Zambelli, Tomaso; Vorholt, Julia A.; Deplancke, Bart

Live-seq 通过微量细胞质取样，在保留细胞活性的同时读取转录组，从而将细胞初始状态与后续分化或刺激响应联系起来。Live-seq samples small amounts of cytoplasm while preserving cell viability, linking an initial transcriptome to later differentiation or responses to stimulation.

Nature Communications 10, 1809 · 2019

ZFP30 如何通过增强子调控脂肪分化ZFP30 promotes adipogenesis through the KAP1-mediated activation of a retrotransposon-derived Pparg2 enhancer

Chen, Wanze; Schwalie, Petra C.; Pankevich, Eugenia V.; Gubelmann, Carine; Raghav, Sunil K.; Dainese, Riccardo; Cassano, Marco; Imbeault, Michael; Jang, Suk Min; Russeil, Julie; Delessa, Tenagne; Duc, Julien; Trono, Didier; Wolfrum, Christian; Deplancke, Bart

研究发现 ZFP30 招募 KAP1，激活来源于逆转座子的 Pparg2 增强子并促进脂肪分化，揭示这一调控复合物在特定细胞背景下的转录激活作用。ZFP30 recruits KAP1 to activate a retrotransposon-derived Pparg2 enhancer and promote adipogenesis, revealing a context-dependent activating role for this regulatory complex.
